Screen filters (Settings > Graphics > Screen Filter)

The filters are drawn by DinoTale's RT64 patch (lib-patches/rt64/0017), in
src/shaders/VideoInterfacePS.hlsl. They are ported from these shaders:

- CRT: from Timothy Lottes' CRT shader (libretro glsl-shaders crt-lottes),
  public domain ("PUBLIC DOMAIN CRT STYLED SCAN-LINE SHADER"), flat (the tube
  curvature is left out). Ported for the Turok: Rage Wars port.
- LCD: from LCD3x by Gigaherz (libretro glsl-shaders lcd3x), public domain.
- Scanlines: written for the Turok: Rage Wars port.
- xBR: Hyllian's xBR-lv2 (libretro glsl-shaders xbr/shaders/xbr-lv2.glsl).
- xBRZ: xBRZ freescale (libretro glsl-shaders xbrz/shaders/xbrz-freescale.glsl).
  The xBRZ algorithm is from the xBRZ scaler by Zenju, GNU General Public
  License v3 (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0).
- HQ2x: Lior Halphon's HQ2x (libretro glsl-shaders hqx/shader-files/hq2x-halphon.glsl).
